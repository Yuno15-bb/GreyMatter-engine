The planet graph_export — a 3D globe of your notes and the links between them
brain (CLI) status · review · selftest · update — every automatic step, runnable by hand

Two layers, on purpose. Layer 1 loses knowledge if it fails, so it always runs, in a fixed order, and the gardener is gated on the distiller actually succeeding. Layer 2 is upkeep: it costs tokens, so it wakes at most one agent per session, and only one with measurable work waiting.
The sensors are mechanical. Dead links, orphans, cold notes, strong overlaps — counted without a single model call, then compared to a threshold. A healthy tree wakes nobody.
